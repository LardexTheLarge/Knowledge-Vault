# Friday journal

Long week. The launch plan is finally written down instead of living in four
different documents, which means I can stop rehearsing it in the shower.

I keep circling the same worry: we are building a tool that asks people to trust
us with the most personal writing they do. That is a strange thing to sell. The
only honest position is to make the trust visible, not to promise it in a
marketing line and then quietly upload everything.

Had lunch with Sam. She asked what happens if the company fails. I said the files
are on disk, so the product failing does not take the notes with it. She said that
was the first answer that made her relax. Worth remembering for the launch post.

Tired in a way that is not about sleep. I think it is the beta list — forty people
is small enough that every rejection feels personal. Need to hand that to Jonas
and stop refreshing the spreadsheet.

Tomorrow: no laptop until noon. Walk, then the market, then read something that is
not a competitor teardown.
