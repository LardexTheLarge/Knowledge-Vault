Not for sharing. Numbers only, no commentary I would want a co-founder to read out
of context.

## If the launch underperforms

1. Cut the studio office share in September; saves 380 EUR a month.
2. Pause the paid tooling (design, analytics) for two months; saves 140 EUR a month.
3. Take the two consulting days a month that I keep turning down. That extends
   runway by about five months on its own.

## The rule

If revenue is below 500 EUR a month by October, start consulting in November.
Decision on 1 October with real numbers, not with anxiety.
